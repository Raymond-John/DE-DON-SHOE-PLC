About DE-DON SHOE PLC

A footwear manufacturing and sales company rooted in Aba, Abia State — built on craftsmanship, consistency and a belief that Nigerian-made shoes can stand with the best.

Who We Are

We make shoes.
That is the work.

DE-DON SHOE PLC is based at Victory Line Shoe Plaza, Aba, Abia State. We design, manufacture and sell footwear directly to customers across Nigeria.

Every pair begins with a pattern and a cut of leather. From there, it moves through stitching, lasting and finishing — steps carried out by people who understand that a shoe is only as good as its construction.

We do not claim a long history, awards or certifications we do not have. What we offer is straightforward: considered footwear, made in Aba, sold directly to you.
