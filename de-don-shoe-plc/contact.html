Inside Our Workshop

The machines
behind each pair.

A shoe is not just cut and shaped by hand — it goes through real machines. This is the equipment we work with in Aba.

Our workshop runs on a small but essential set of machines. They don't replace the hands — they give the hands leverage, precision and consistency.

  • Sewing Machine

    Used to stitch leather uppers, aprons and linings together with waxed thread. Runs every day.

  • Skiving Machine

    Thins the edges of leather so folds sit flat and seams are clean.

  • Lasting Machine

    Pulls the upper over the last and shapes the final fit of the shoe.

  • Finishing Tools

    Burnishing wheels and polishing brushes finish edges and soles before packing.
